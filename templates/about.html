How Data is Processed

1

URL Submission

When you submit a URL, it's first validated and sanitized to prevent injection attacks. We check the URL format, length (max 2048 chars), and ensure it contains valid characters.

2

Feature Extraction

Our advanced feature extractor analyzes 50+ characteristics including URL length, special character frequency, domain age, SSL certificate validity, subdomain count, and suspicious keyword detection.

3

Whitelist Check

Before ML analysis, we check against our whitelist of 300+ verified legitimate domains (Google, Facebook, Microsoft, etc.). Whitelisted domains are instantly marked as safe.

4

ML Model Analysis

The extracted features are passed through our trained Scikit-learn model which uses pattern recognition to classify the URL. The model outputs a prediction (Safe/Phishing) and confidence score (0-100%).

5

External Verification (Optional)

For enhanced accuracy, we cross-reference with external APIs like Google Safe Browsing and VirusTotal. This multi-source validation increases confidence from 60% to 95%+ when sources agree.

6

Risk Assessment

We analyze risk factors (new domain, no HTTPS, suspicious TLD) and trust factors (established domain, valid SSL, legitimate registrar) to calculate a final risk level: Low, Medium, or High.

7

Results & Caching

Results are displayed instantly with detailed analysis. For registered users, scans are saved to history. Results are cached for 24 hours to speed up repeated scans of the same URL.

Your Privacy & Data Security

We take your privacy seriously. Here's how we handle your data:

URLs are not stored for anonymous users
Scan history saved only for registered users
Passwords encrypted with bcrypt hashing
Secure HTTPS connections enforced
No third-party data sharing
API keys are hashed and secured
Sessions expire after inactivity
Rate limiting prevents abuse

Data Retention: Scan history is retained for registered users until account deletion. Anonymous scans are processed in real-time and immediately discarded. Cache data expires after 24 hours.
